# Announcements to leaders: drafts for you to read, change and send

Paste each into **Admin → Announcements → New announcement**, choose the **Leaders** audience, and send it through the usual approval. Nothing here is sent by the platform by itself. Change anything you like: the wording is yours.

## 1. Foundation of Faith: waived this season only

**Subject:** Foundation of Faith: not required to lead this season

> Hello,
>
> A short note about Foundation of Faith. For this season only, you do not need to have finished it to serve as a tribe leader, or to be asked to lead. If you have not done it yet, nothing is wrong and nobody is waiting on you.
>
> It will be asked for again from the next season, so if you can, please book the next class when it is announced.
>
> Thank you for leading.

**Before you send it:** make the rule match the words. Open **Admin → Leader pipeline**, find **Foundation of Faith**, and set it to **Optional**, not Off, so the question is still asked, a "no" does not stop anyone, and the answers are kept. Set it back to **Required** when the next season opens.

## 2. When the next week's guide opens (send once, when you turn the Saturday release On)

**Subject:** Next week's guide opens on Saturday

> Hello,
>
> From now on, each week's guide opens for you on the Saturday before it starts, at 8 am. Until then, **This week** shows this week's guide and the weeks before it, and a later week tells you when it opens.
>
> You will always find next week's guide waiting for you on the Saturday, in time to read it before you meet.
>
> If anything looks wrong, tell your coach.

If you choose a different time on **Admin → Weekly guides**, change "8 am" here to match.

## 3. A note for week 2 (optional)

**Subject:** Week 2: what to bring

> Hello,
>
> This week your tribe meets for the second time. Ask each person how their step went, then read the guide for the week on **This week**. A short, warm call to anyone who missed last week is worth more than a long message.
>
> If you have a question, the **How to use this app** button on the Lead page now has a short page called **Questions leaders ask**.
>
> Thank you for being there for your tribe.

## 4. A heads-up before the seat rules are switched on (send before you turn **Seat window** On)

**Subject:** What is changing for members who have not answered

> Hello,
>
> When the church places someone in your tribe, they now have 48 hours to say they will come. If they have not answered by then, the seat is offered to the next person on the waiting list, and the person who did not answer is told kindly that they can take it back for seven days.
>
> You will get one short note a day, at 5 pm, only on days when something changed. It tells you who said yes, and how many seats opened or are still waiting. It never names someone who was released and never gives a reason.
>
> Nobody is moved out of your tribe because of anything you did or did not do. If you want to keep a seat for someone, tell your coach or the office.

Only send this one if you are going to turn the **Seat window** on.
