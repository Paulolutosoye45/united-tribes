# Weeks 1 to 3: what a leader reads, for the pastor to approve or mark

This is read straight from the database, so it is exactly the words a leader sees on **This week** for each of the first three weeks, in the order the page shows them. Nothing has been changed or tidied. Teaching wording is yours to judge: mark what to change, and an admin edits it under **Admin → Weekly guides**. Editing a guide changes it for every tribe at once.

For each week: tick **Approved**, or write the change beside the line. (An approved week is also marked as read on the Weekly guides page when an admin opens it and presses the button there.)


## Week 1: Begin with the end in view

- **Theme:** begin
- **Passage:** Habakkuk 2:2–3

**Big idea.** A goal you can see is a goal you can walk toward. Tonight your tribe agrees what success looks like in twelve weeks and writes it down, so everyone can run with it.

**Opening question.** What made you say yes to this tribe?

**Questions for the tribe**

1. Read the draft Success Picture together. Which line excites you, and which feels too big or too small?
1. What would make you say, in week 12, that this season was worth it?
1. What is the one thing most likely to get in the way, and how can we help each other past it?

**The practice.** Write your own goal for the season in one sentence and keep it where you will see it every day.

**An idea for the week's step** (shown to members). When I pick up my phone in the morning, I will read my season goal aloud before I open WhatsApp.

**The prayer.** Pray for each person's goal, one at a time. Ask God for clarity and courage for the season.

**The plan for the meeting (75 minutes)**

1. **Arrive and check in** (10 minutes). Keep it light and warm. Ask for one win and one hard thing from the week. From week 2, ask who did their step and what happened. Do not rush this part.
2. **The Word** (15 minutes). Read Habakkuk 2:2–3 aloud. Ask one person to say in their own words why God tells us to write the vision down and to wait for it.
3. **Talk it through** (25 minutes). Refine the Success Picture together. Read each line (Loving God, Loving Others, Loving Life), change the words until everyone can say it, and save the final version on the platform this week. Then agree how you will reach each other between meetings.
4. **Choose this week's step** (15 minutes). Each person finishes the sentence "When ___, I will ___" and says it aloud. Keep it small enough to do on your worst day. They save it on the platform.
5. **Pray for one another** (10 minutes). Pray for each person's goal, one at a time. Ask God for clarity and courage for the season.

**A word for the leader.** Keep tonight warm and light, because people are deciding whether this tribe is safe. Share your own goal first and honestly so others can follow. Remind everyone that what is said here stays here.

**The line above the guide (week layer)**

- Confession: I write the vision and make it plain. God's appointed time for it will come and will not delay, so I will run with it.
- Sunday sermon: (not set)
- Skill-share prompt for the tribe's community: Name one skill at work you are proud of and one you want to grow. Ask the tribe who has the one you want.
- Goal idea for the tribe's goal: Name the leadership role you want and the three skills it needs. Rate yourself honestly on each.

## Week 2: Hear Him every day

- **Theme:** loving_god
- **Passage:** Joshua 1:8; Psalm 1:1–3

**Big idea.** A strong season is built on small daily habits with God. Ten honest minutes with Him each morning does more than one long prayer once a week.

**Opening question.** Where and when do you usually meet with God, if you do?

**Questions for the tribe**

1. What gets in the way of a daily quiet time for you: early starts, traffic, a shared room, notifications, tiredness?
1. What is the smallest daily habit with God you could keep even on a hard day?
1. When has a verse or a quiet moment with God changed how you handled a day?

**The practice.** Choose a fixed time and place for ten minutes with God on five days this week.

**An idea for the week's step** (shown to members). When I get into the car or the bus in the morning, I will read one chapter on my phone and pray for two minutes.

**The prayer.** Pray for a hunger for God's Word in each person, and for protection over the time they set aside.

**The plan for the meeting (75 minutes)**

1. **Arrive and check in** (10 minutes). Keep it light and warm. Ask for one win and one hard thing from the week. From week 2, ask who did their step and what happened. Do not rush this part.
2. **The Word** (15 minutes). Read Joshua 1:8 and Psalm 1:1–3. Ask: what does it mean that the person in Psalm 1 is like a tree planted by water?
3. **Talk it through** (25 minutes). Share real routines and do not compare. Each person names one small habit, one fixed time and one place. Pair up and say it to each other.
4. **Choose this week's step** (15 minutes). Each person finishes the sentence "When ___, I will ___" and says it aloud. Keep it small enough to do on your worst day. They save it on the platform.
5. **Pray for one another** (10 minutes). Pray for a hunger for God's Word in each person, and for protection over the time they set aside.

**A word for the leader.** Many members are tired and busy. Praise small steps and never make anyone feel behind. Share a time you missed many days and came back.

**The line above the guide (week layer)**

- Confession: I delight in the Word of God and think on it day and night. I am like a tree planted by streams of water, and what I do will prosper.

- Goal idea for the tribe's goal: Name the leadership role you want and the three skills it needs. Rate yourself honestly on each.

## Week 3: Pray with expectation, not performance

- **Theme:** loving_god
- **Passage:** Philippians 4:6–7

**Big idea.** Prayer is talking to a Father who listens, not a speech to perform. We bring the real worries, the bills, the interview, the family, and ask Him for peace and help.

**Opening question.** What is one thing you have been carrying alone this week?

**Questions for the tribe**

1. What worry keeps coming back when you try to pray, and what do you do with it?
1. What is the difference between praying about something and worrying about it?
1. Tell about a time God answered, even if it came late or looked different from what you asked.

**The practice.** Each day, write one request and one thing you are thankful for. Look back on Friday.

**An idea for the week's step** (shown to members). When I switch off the light at night, I will write one prayer request and one thank-you in my notes.

**The prayer.** Pray short, honest prayers for the one need each person has just shared. One or two sentences each.

**The plan for the meeting (75 minutes)**

1. **Arrive and check in** (10 minutes). Keep it light and warm. Ask for one win and one hard thing from the week. From week 2, ask who did their step and what happened. Do not rush this part.
2. **The Word** (15 minutes). Read Philippians 4:6–7 slowly, twice. Ask each person to name the word or phrase that stands out to them.
3. **Talk it through** (25 minutes). Take two or three real needs from the tribe, with permission, and pray for them tonight instead of only talking about prayer. Keep all details inside the tribe.
4. **Choose this week's step** (15 minutes). Each person finishes the sentence "When ___, I will ___" and says it aloud. Keep it small enough to do on your worst day. They save it on the platform.
5. **Pray for one another** (10 minutes). Pray short, honest prayers for the one need each person has just shared. One or two sentences each.

**A word for the leader.** Prayer needs can be heavy. If someone shares something that sounds like harm to themselves or someone else, follow the safeguarding steps from your training and speak to your coach the same day.

**The line above the guide (week layer)**

- Confession: I will not be anxious about anything. In everything I pray with thanksgiving, and the peace of God guards my heart and my mind in Christ Jesus.

- Goal idea for the tribe's goal: Name the leadership role you want and the three skills it needs. Rate yourself honestly on each.
